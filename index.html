About Arthanisa

Meaning before measurement.

ARTHANISA brings architecture and interior design together as one practice. The studio was founded by Ar. Meher Chouparne and co-founded by Ar. Sidhu Shaikh, with an approach centred on simplicity, warmth and responsible design.

This is the introduction only. The full story, visionaries, team and institutional work live on the About Us page — so the home page does not repeat the same content.
