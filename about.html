About Arthanisa

The story behind the studio.

ARTHANISA, the word, has its roots in Sanskrit — an amalgamation of “Arth”, meaning meaningful, and “Nisa”, which translates to home. The studio’s belief is to create meaningful, niche spaces in people’s lives.

ARTHANISA is the brainchild of Ar. Meher Chouparne, co-founded by Ar. Sidhu Shaikh, based in Thane, Mumbai. The practice brings planning, designing, detailing and liaison services together under one roof.

The studio’s approach prioritises simplicity, warmth and responsible design, providing architectural and interior design solutions regardless of the scale and size of the project.
