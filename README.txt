ARTHANISA REVISED WEBSITE
==========================

Files:
- index.html
- about.html
- projects.html
- contact.html
- style.css
- script.js

Main changes:
1. One compact MENU bar on every viewport; links open in a full navigation panel.
2. Header is always visible; after scrolling it becomes transparent/translucent.
3. LIGHT GREY theme across the entire site — no black or dark background anywhere.
   Base page grey #ECECEA, alternating bands and footer #E1E1DE, cards/tiles #F4F4F2.
   Body text #26251F, secondary text #5E5C55, accent gold deepened to #9A7A22 so it
   stays legible on a light background. Header, hero, CTA band and footer are all light.
4. Home and About content is separated so the same story is not repeated.
5. About page follows the wireframe: About Arthanisa, Vision, Visionaries, Our Team, Our Expertise, Empanelment, Get in Touch.
6. Empanelment includes MMRDA, TMC, PWD, MHADA and Mumbai Port Authority (MbPA).
7. Projects follow the wireframe categories: Architecture, Interiors, Landscaping.
8. Project layouts use the supplied StudioMAT-style reference: two project-image blocks, brief/metadata, and a location-map block.
9. Project details are deliberately marked as placeholders where the uploaded material did not provide the final client-approved title, images, client or completion year.
10. Contact page follows the supplied contact wireframe.
